Pocket Bonsai privacy policy

Effective 11 October 2026

Pocket Bonsai collects no data. Your tree lives on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app doesn't send anything to us or to anyone else.

Who we are

Pocket Bonsai is made by Wakalo ("we"). This policy covers the Pocket Bonsai app for iPhone and its widgets.

What stays on your device

To grow your tree, the app keeps a small file on your iPhone with:

  • your tree's name and species, and the number that makes your tree unique;
  • the days you watered it and your choices about new shoots;
  • your settings (reminder time, seasonal notes, hemisphere, haptics) and the small moments shown in Rings.

This file is shared only with the app's own widgets on the same device. We never receive it. It may be included in your iPhone's backups (for example iCloud Backup), which Apple handles under your own settings.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no server, no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting services, no advertising and no third-party code that collects data. We don't track you across apps or websites.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders or seasonal notes, the app schedules them on your iPhone using Apple's local notifications. Nothing is sent over the internet, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in the Settings app.

Sharing a postcard

When you share a postcard of your tree, the app hands the picture and its caption to the iOS share sheet, and you choose where it goes. What happens next is up to the app or person you share it with.

Children

Pocket Bonsai is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your tree and everything listed above from your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If the app ever started collecting data, we would say so here and in the App Store before it did.
