Common questions

Can my tree die? No. If you're away, it rests, and it's there when you come back.

How do I water from the widget? Add the medium Pocket Bonsai widget to your home screen and tap Water.

The seasons look wrong. Seasons follow your hemisphere. You can change it in the app's Settings.

How do I turn reminders on or off? In the app's Settings, under Reminder.
